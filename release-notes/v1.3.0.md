# TypeType for Android 1.3.0

I am very happy to share TypeType for Android 1.3.0.

This is the biggest Android update so far. The application is still in beta,
but this release brings it much closer to the experience and features available
in the TypeType web application.

## What is new

- Native Shorts with swipe navigation, playback controls and preloading.
- Better playback recovery after network changes, interrupted requests and
  expired SABR sessions.
- Improved seeking, live playback, background playback and Picture-in-Picture.
- Audio-only playback, which can now be selected as the default.
- Media controls through notifications, the lock screen, headsets and
  Picture-in-Picture.
- Server-provided subtitles with styling and caption appearance settings.
- SponsorBlock controls, chapters and clearer skip feedback.
- DeArrow titles and thumbnails across supported video surfaces.
- Bullet comments with position and display settings.
- Native registration and OIDC login through an external browser.
- YouTube session pairing and resumable YouTube Takeout imports.
- Better channel pages, search filters, subscriptions and pagination.
- Watch history filters and visible Continue Watching progress.
- More complete content, privacy, playback and appearance settings.
- Improved tablet layouts, accessibility and large-text support.
- Faster startup through generated baseline and startup profiles.
- More resilient downloads, account restoration and process recreation.

TypeType for Android remains compatible with Android 6.0 through Android 17 and
does not require Google Play Services.

Some features depend on the capabilities exposed by your TypeType-Server
instance. If something does not work correctly, please open an issue in the
[TypeType-Android repository](https://github.com/TypeType-Video/TypeType-Android/issues).

The attached APK is built from the tagged source, signed by the release
workflow, checked for 16 KiB page alignment and verified against the Gradle
application version. A SHA-256 checksum is provided alongside it.

Thank you to everyone testing the Android beta and reporting problems. Sharing
TypeType is one of the best ways to support the project. Sponsorships also help
cover the servers, domains and coffee that keep development moving.
