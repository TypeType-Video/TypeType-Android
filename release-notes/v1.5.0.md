# TypeType for Android 1.5.0

TypeType for Android 1.5.0 brings a much more complete Shorts experience,
deeper player interactions and a long list of improvements across browsing,
comments, subscriptions and self-hosted instances.

The application is still in beta. Thank you to everyone testing it, reporting
problems and helping improve the Android experience.

## What is new

- A rebuilt Shorts experience with smoother transitions, faster startup and
  more reliable playback while scrolling.
- Swipe from a Short to open its channel, with clearer gesture feedback and
  controls that remain available during the interaction.
- Copy titles, view engagement details and use more complete playback controls
  directly from Shorts.
- Faster transitions between prepared videos through playback-session and
  stream reuse.
- Comments are now available from the player quick actions.
- Load paginated comment replies, including nested conversations, with clearer
  timestamps, likes, loading states and errors.
- More natural player gestures for seeking, volume and brightness, with saved
  brightness preferences.
- Expanded fullscreen controls and more reliable restoration when moving
  between embedded, expanded and fullscreen playback.
- Better handling of system bars, orientation changes and playback ending
  while fullscreen is active.
- Smoother player expansion and collapse with continuous motion during drag
  gestures.
- Subscription feeds refresh without hiding useful cached content during a
  temporary failure.
- Stronger separation between accounts and instances when subscriptions are
  cached or refreshed.
- More useful diagnostics when a subscription page cannot be loaded.
- Search, top-level navigation and restored application state now stay in sync
  more reliably.
- Cleaner layouts for channels, playlists, podcasts, notifications, settings
  and player details.
- Corrected playlist and video counts, live replay identification, shared URLs
  and video menu state.
- TypeType can now trust certificate authorities installed by the user, making
  private and locally hosted HTTPS instances easier to use.
- Refreshed authentication cookies are persisted before the application exits,
  reducing unnecessary sign-ins after process recreation.
- New performance coverage for Shorts paging and playback prefetch behavior.

Features provided by newer TypeType-Server versions appear automatically when
they are available. Older instances remain usable without showing unsupported
controls.

TypeType for Android supports Android 6.0 through Android 17 and does not
require Google Play Services.

If you encounter a problem, please open an issue in the
[TypeType-Android repository](https://github.com/TypeType-Video/TypeType-Android/issues).
