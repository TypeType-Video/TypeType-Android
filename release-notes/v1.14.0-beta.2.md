TypeType Android 1.14.0-beta.2

- Watch a Twitch live with its chat under the video, or beside it on a wide screen, instead of a sheet over the video.
- See above the chat how many people watch, how long the live has been on, its category, and follow the channel.
- See Twitch, BTTV, FFZ and 7TV emotes, badges and the colors of names in the live chat and in the chat replay of past broadcasts.
- Keep the last chat lines and the message field in view while typing.
- Replay the chat of a past broadcast beside the video.
- Find the past broadcasts of the channels you follow and the channels themselves on the Live tab.
- Load Twitch pictures and emotes through your instance, as the web does.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on.

I tested this beta on an Android 15 emulator against server 1.19.0-rc.2 with a linked Twitch account.
