TypeType Android 1.14.0-beta.1

- Add a Live tab with the Twitch lives of the moment, their categories and a channel search.
- Watch Twitch lives, past broadcasts and clips, and open Twitch channel pages with their videos and clips.
- Show how many people watch a live.
- Read the Twitch chat of a live, with its polls and pinned messages.
- Replay the chat of Twitch past broadcasts, and jump through their game chapters.
- Start a Twitch live over from its beginning.
- Connect your Twitch account in Settings > Linked accounts, follow channels, see the followed channels that are live, send chat messages and vote in polls.
- Choose when a video counts as watched, and hide watched videos in Subscriptions, in Settings > Content.
- Show home recommendations as one grid or by category.
- Play Bilibili and NicoNico lives again.
- Say when a Twitch stream has ended.

Twitch needs server 1.18 with Twitch turned on. The watched settings need a server newer than 1.18.0-rc.7.

I tested this beta against servers 1.18.0-rc.7 and 1.18.0-beta.357. I could not test connecting a Twitch account to the end, or following channels, sending messages and voting with one, so tell me if something goes wrong there.
