TypeType Android 1.11.0-beta.9

- Recommendations now have their own entry in Settings, with the same sections as the web app: overview, algorithm, guide, data used, what it keeps, details and your opinions.
- See what your home page is made of and the share of each interest, then open the setting behind any part of the chart.
- Review the data the algorithm reads, limit how far back it reads, and exclude single videos.
- Pin, hide or stop counting an interest or a channel, and forget recently watched videos.
- See every setting with its default value, and how the last home page was built.
- When the instance has the recommender but its admin has not offered it yet, Settings now explains it instead of staying silent.
