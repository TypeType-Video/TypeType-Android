TypeType Android 1.10.0-beta.3

- Choose the default video quality locally on your device, with Auto and 144p
  through 2160p.
- Cap automatic playback on mobile data at 1080p by default, with a
  configurable limit.
- Keep quality changes on the device instead of synchronizing them to the
  server.
- Apply the same local quality policy to regular videos, Shorts, and queued
  playback.
- Includes the playback recovery improvements from 1.10.0-beta.2.
