TypeType Android 1.12.0-beta.2

- Play NicoNico videos again: the app now builds one HLS playlist for the video qualities and the audio, as the web app does, and audio-only mode works again for them.
- Load Bilibili covers and avatars at the size they are shown, instead of the full images (up to 400 KB each).
- Stop the player from switching in and out of fullscreen on the inner screen of large foldables in landscape: that screen now behaves like a tablet, with no automatic fullscreen when you rotate (the fullscreen button still works). Phones are unchanged.
